Teams are assembled across disciplines on the first day. This is how we know what you bring to one.

03Have you worked with modular or timber construction?

Not a filter. Much of the room will answer “never” — the workshops on the first day exist for exactly that.

04Can you give it the full 48 hours?

It runs from 09:00 on 8 October to 09:00 on 10 October, Bali time (UTC+8), and does not pause overnight. Work out your own timezone first, then answer honestly — half a Modulthon helps nobody.

05Working with anyone?

Alone is the default and the most common answer. Teams are formed on the first day, and most people finish with people they did not start with.
